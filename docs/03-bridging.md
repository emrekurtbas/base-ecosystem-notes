# Bridging to Base

## Official Bridge
- URL: https://bridge.base.org
- Deposits: ~1 minute (Ethereum → Base)
- Withdrawals: ~7 days (Base → Ethereum) due to fraud proof window

## Third-Party Bridges (faster withdrawals)
| Bridge | Fee | Speed |
|--------|-----|-------|
| Across | ~0.05% | Minutes |
| Stargate | ~0.06% | Minutes |
| Relay | ~0.04% | Seconds |
| Synapse | ~0.05% | Minutes |

## Tips
- Use third-party bridges for withdrawals to avoid the 7-day wait
- Always verify bridge contract addresses from official sources
